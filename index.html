Last Stable Orbit

A spinning black hole of 4.3 million suns, like the one at the centre of our galaxy. Its shadow is about 65 million km across. The ring station beside you is 4,000 km wide, and the Earth-sized planet out there is a speck. Every pixel follows a ray of light through curved spacetime.

Drag on the left side to fly and on the right side to look. The buttons climb, dive, boost, let go and fall, show labels or the spacetime panel, take a photo, switch viewpoints and start the tour. Time is fast-forward: at ×169 each real second shows almost 3 minutes at the black hole. Landscape works best.
